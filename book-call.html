How Does the Free Call Work?

In the free discovery call, we take 20 minutes to get to know each other. The call happens online via video – from the comfort of your home.

What happens in the call?

  • You tell me what's going on in your life right now
  • We check if Raw & Real is suitable for your situation
  • I explain how I work and what you can expect
  • You can ask all your questions
  • We see if we're a good fit to work together

Important Note:

The discovery call also serves to check whether Raw & Real is even suitable for your situation. This is lifestyle coaching based on personal experience, NOT medical treatment, nutritional therapy, or psychotherapy.

Amy Lang is a Neue Selbständige (Austria) with no medical or nutritional science degree. If you have acute psychological issues, eating disorders, or need medical/therapeutic support, I will tell you honestly and recommend appropriate professionals.

You must consult your doctor before making significant dietary changes, especially if you have health conditions, take medications, are pregnant, or breastfeeding.

No obligation, completely free: The discovery call is completely non-binding. There's no sales pressure – it's only about finding out if we're a good match and if my work is suitable for you.
