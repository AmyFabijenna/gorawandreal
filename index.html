What Lived Change Can Make Possible

Nicole Vogt

I had tried transformation programmes before. None of them addressed what I truly needed.

With three young children, cooking separately for them while finding my own way, I felt torn. How could I honour what I knew was right for my body while my family lived differently? How could I make room for my own needs again after years of giving everything to everyone else?

Then I found Amy.

She did not give me a one-size-fits-all plan. She showed me how to follow my path while my family followed theirs — with clarity, without conflict and without turning it into a family project.

Through WhatsApp, Monday to Friday, she was always there. No detail was too small. Every uncertainty, every question — she had practical answers. No theory. Real solutions.

When I did not know how to navigate family meals, she showed me how.

When I doubted whether I could truly do it, she gave me her unshakeable belief. That it would work. That I could do it. That it did not have to be hard.

Three months later:

I feel exceptionally fit. Full of energy. Mentally clear. I need less sleep, have more vitality and feel like myself again — truly myself, not only “Mum”.

And then came the moment I will never forget:

Everyone in my family had the flu. Fever. Coughing. The full ordeal.

I was the only one who did not get ill.

My husband — who had always thought my approach was “a bit much” — looked at me and said: “What are you doing differently? I want to try it too.”

Today he asks what I am eating. He is interested. He can see the results in me.

Not because I persuaded him, but because he saw how well I felt. How I looked. How I stayed well while everyone else became ill.

Amy’s belief becomes your own. And suddenly you no longer doubt — you simply know that it works.

Before You Take the First Step

Who is this guidance for?
For women who are not looking for more self-optimisation, but for a life that feels more beautiful, alive, clear and unmistakably their own.
What role does nutrition play?
The body is not a side issue. Living nutrition creates a foundation of freshness, energy, pleasure and radiance upon which other changes can take form more naturally.
What does living nutrition mean?
Natural, plant-based foods; colour, freshness, variety and harmonious rhythms. Raw food and food combining may be included without becoming a rigid system.
What does shamanic process guidance mean?
Images from nature, rituals, stories and symbols become pathways of perception and personal reflection. They open new perspectives without imposing a ready-made truth upon you.
How is this offering defined legally?
This offering comprises general education, knowledge-sharing and personal reflection. Medical diagnosis and treatment, psychotherapy and regulated nutritional counselling remain the responsibility of the legally authorised professions.
