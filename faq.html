About the Program

Do I need to be 100% raw from day one?

No. We work step by step. You decide the pace. I've been through every challenge myself – I know what works and what doesn't. Some people transition quickly, others take it slow. Both approaches work.

What if my family eats cooked food?

I'll show you how to cook for them while eating raw yourself – without making two separate meals. Nicole did it with three kids. You can too. Most of my clients have families who eat cooked food. It's completely doable.

Do I need cooking skills or special equipment?

No special skills needed. I'll teach you everything from scratch. Equipment-wise, you'll eventually want a dehydrator (for raw bread/crackers) and a good blender, but we can start with what you have at home.

What if I fail again?

You won't. Because you won't be alone this time. I'm thorough. I leave no detail unaddressed. And I transfer my belief onto you – so you know it works. Every challenge you face, I've faced it too. I know the solutions.

About Feng Shui & Soul Work

What's included in the Feng Shui analysis?

A complete analysis of your home – room by room, family member by family member. You'll get a detailed report with concrete optimization suggestions you can implement immediately. This helps create a space that supports your transformation.

What is "soul work" exactly?

It's the inner work that goes beyond food. We address doubts, beliefs, emotional blocks – whatever is holding you back. Some of my tools come from shamanic practice, but you don't need to believe in anything specific. It's practical, not theoretical.

Practical Questions

What results can I expect?

Results vary significantly by individual. Many of my clients report increased energy, better sleep, clearer skin, and natural weight release when their body is ready. The focus is on sustainable lifestyle changes, not quick fixes. Your results depend on your starting point, commitment, and individual circumstances.

Will I be hungry all the time?

No. Raw food is high-volume, low-calorie. You'll eat more volume than before, but fewer calories. Most clients are surprised by how much they can eat. I'll teach you how to eat enough to feel satisfied.

What about social situations and eating out?

We'll work on strategies for restaurants, parties, and family gatherings. It's completely doable. Most of my clients maintain a social life while eating raw. You just need to know how.

Is this expensive (food costs)?

Raw food can be as cheap or expensive as you make it. Seasonal fruits and vegetables are affordable. Exotic superfoods are optional. I'll show you how to do this on any budget.
